1. Data controller

Hadona Digital Media, Jakarta, Indonesia, is the controller of personal data collected through this website. Contact: hello@hadona.id.

2. What we collect

  • Contact data you submit: name, work email, company or website, monthly budget range, and your message.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, referring source.
  • Service data: advertising account identifiers and performance data you share with us to deliver the engagement.

3. Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare an audit or proposal.
  • To deliver contracted services and report on performance.
  • To maintain security, prevent spam and abuse, and debug the site.
  • To comply with legal obligations and, where permitted, send updates you asked for.

4. Legal basis

For enquiries we rely on legitimate interest in responding to a business request, and on steps taken at your request before a contract. For marketing email we rely on consent, which you can withdraw at any time. For service delivery we rely on contract performance. For security and compliance we rely on legal obligation and legitimate interest.

5. Processors and third parties

We use a small set of processors, each bound by a data processing agreement where required:

  • Vercel for website hosting and edge delivery.
  • Resend for transactional email delivery.
  • Supabase for enquiry records.
  • Cloudflare R2 for media storage.
  • Analytics providers, where enabled, for aggregate traffic measurement.

6. Cookies and analytics

The site uses strictly necessary cookies to function. Analytics cookies, if enabled, measure aggregate traffic and are not used to build advertising profiles of visitors. You can block or delete cookies in your browser; blocking necessary cookies may affect functionality.

7. International transfers

Processors may store data outside Indonesia, including in the EU, the United States and Asia-Pacific. Where personal data leaves the EEA, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent lawful mechanism.

8. Retention

Enquiry records are kept for up to 24 months after the last contact, unless we enter a contract, in which case records are kept for the period required for tax, accounting and contractual purposes. Server logs are kept for a short operational period.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request erasure where there is no overriding obligation to keep it.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

10. Security

We apply access controls, encrypted transport, least-privilege access to client accounts and vendor due diligence. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

11. Contact

For any privacy request, email hello@hadona.id with the subject line "Privacy request". We respond within 30 days.
