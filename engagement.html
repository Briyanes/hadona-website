Three ways to work

Pick by problem, not by budget

01 / Growth retainer

We run the account.

Full ownership of paid media, creative supply and measurement. One media buyer, one creative producer, one account lead. This is the default engagement and covers most brands.

  • Daily buying and optimisation
  • 4–8 new creative assets per month
  • Pixel, CAPI and feed maintenance
  • Live dashboard plus weekly written review
  • Monthly strategy session

Best for: USD 1,000–20,000 monthly media spend

02 / Rebuild sprint

Four weeks, fixed scope.

An audit and rebuild without a long commitment. For accounts that were never structured properly, or teams taking media in-house.

  • Tracking and event audit
  • Account restructure
  • Creative testing framework
  • Handover document

Best for: first-time engagements

03 / Advisory

Your team runs it. We read it.

For in-house teams that need a second pair of eyes every month rather than another vendor.

  • Monthly account review
  • Budget and structure feedback
  • Dashboard setup
  • Async questions

Best for: teams above USD 20,000 monthly spend

The first month

Ten working days to live

Week 0 — Access and audit +

Ad account admin, pixel, catalogue and analytics access. We read the last 90 days, check tracking against the bank statement, and write down what is broken before recommending anything.

Week 1 — Structure and tracking +

Event mapping fixed, CAPI where relevant, account rebuilt into campaigns with one job each. Spend stays flat this week on purpose: there is no point scaling a broken measurement setup.

Week 2 — First campaigns live +

New creative in market, budget paced, stop-losses set. You get the dashboard link and the first written note on what we expect to see.

Week 3–4 — First read +

A real read on cost per result, not a victory lap. If the numbers are not moving yet, you hear that in week four rather than month three.

Before you sign

Straight answers

How much does it cost? +

The retainer depends on scope: channels, creative volume and how much of the account needs rebuilding. We quote after the audit, not before, because a number given without looking is a guess. Media budget starts at USD 1,000 per month and is paid to the platforms, not to us.

Do we have to sign a long contract? +

Three months initially, because the first month is measurement and structure and judging before that is judging noise. After that it runs monthly with 30 days notice.

Who owns the ad accounts and the data? +

You do. We work inside your accounts, under your business verification. If we part ways, the history, audiences and creative stay with you.

Do you work with brands outside Indonesia? +

Yes. Most of our work is for brands selling in Indonesia and Southeast Asia, and we run remote engagements for brands based elsewhere. If your market is one we have not operated in, we say so before quoting.

What happens if it does not work? +

You get a written read every month, including the months that are flat. If the account is not moving by month three, we tell you what we would change and you decide whether to continue. We do not offer money-back guarantees; we offer a reporting standard you can hold us to.

How do we start? +

Send the account details through the contact form. We reply within one business day with two or three questions and, if it looks like a fit, a time for a 30-minute call.
