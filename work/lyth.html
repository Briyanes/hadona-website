LYTH Outdoor

A premium outdoor label with a five-step checkout and no story. We rebuilt the store around how the product is actually worn, then removed every step between wanting it and owning it.

+65%Conversion rate, 1.2% → 2.0%
82% → 47%Cart abandonment
+38%Average order value

The challenge

LYTH Outdoor sells premium technical garments under the line "attitude in hybrid form". The product was strong; the store was not. Traffic arrived and left.

  • Bounce rate sat at 75 percent, with a store that was not built mobile first.
  • Checkout took five or more steps, and 82 percent of carts were abandoned.
  • Product photography was flat, so buyers could not judge fit, texture or movement.
  • Repeat purchase rate was 18 percent, with nothing happening after the first order.

What we changed

1. Show the garment moving

Every product gained multiple angles, a texture close-up and a fifteen-second clip showing fit and movement. Lifestyle photography placed the pieces in real weather rather than on a seamless backdrop.

2. Two steps to checkout

Checkout was cut to two steps with guest checkout available, QRIS and e-wallet added alongside cards, and abandoned carts recovered over WhatsApp instead of email alone.

3. A brand that reads as one thing

An earth-tone palette, consistent typography and microcopy written in the brand's own voice replaced a mix of marketplace assets. A "The LYTH Story" section explains materials and construction, which is what justifies the price.

Results

2.0%Conversion rate, from 1.2%
Rp442.000Order value, from Rp320.000
34%Repeat purchase, from 18%

Mobile conversion rose 120 percent, session duration doubled from 1m21s to 2m47s, and monthly revenue averaged 78 percent higher after launch. Cart abandonment fell to 47 percent, still above where we want it.

What we would do next

  • Push abandonment below 35 percent with a one-tap wallet checkout.
  • Build a loyalty tier for outerwear buyers, who return on a seasonal cycle.
  • Feed customer photography back into paid social as the primary creative source.
