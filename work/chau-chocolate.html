Cau Chocolates

A premium chocolate brand competing in Shopee's discount calendar. We stopped bidding every day and spent where the marketplace was actually buying.

1.45xROAS over two months
Rp122.010Average order value, +53%
Rp84.034Cost per purchase

The challenge

Cau Chocolates sells premium chocolate on Shopee, where the calendar is dominated by 8.8, 9.9 and payday campaigns. Similar products are discounted by as much as 70 percent during those windows. Outside them, the account was barely converting.

  • Average order value outside event periods sat at Rp80.000, against Rp122.010 inside them.
  • There was no acquisition ceiling, so a sale could cost more than the margin it produced.
  • Spend was spread evenly across the month instead of concentrated where demand existed.

What we changed

1. Budget followed the calendar

We moved 70 percent of the budget into the 8.8, 9.9 and payday windows, and kept the rest as a thin always-on layer for product page traffic. Targeting was narrowed to premium chocolate buyers aged 25 to 45, plus shoppers who had previously bought comparable products on the platform.

2. One creative idea per event

Twin-date events ran a gifting angle with a 20 percent discount. Payday ran a self-reward angle with free shipping. Same product, different reason to buy, which kept frequency from burning the same audience twice.

3. A cost ceiling below the order value

Cost per acquisition was capped at Rp90.000, deliberately under the event order value of Rp122.010. Auto-bidding was only allowed to prioritise conversion during peak hours, so the account did not chase cheap clicks at midnight.

Results

Two months, 28 August to 23 October 2025. Figures come from the client's own Shopee and campaign reporting.

Revenue reached Rp14,5 million against Rp84.034 cost per purchase, which kept every sale profitable. The 53 percent lift in average order value came mostly from bundling two products into the event creative rather than discounting a single unit harder.

What we would do next

  • Build an always-on bundle for non-event weeks to hold order value above Rp100.000.
  • Test a second marketplace to reduce dependence on one discount calendar.
  • Add a repeat-purchase reminder at day 30, since chocolate reorders cluster around gifting moments.
