What we need from you

The honest list

Access +

Ad account admin, pixel, and analytics. We work inside your accounts, not in a parallel account that hides history.

Creative inputs +

Product, packaging, and one person on your side who can approve assets within 48 hours. Slow approval is the most common reason creative goes stale.

Budget +

At least USD 1,000 per month in media spend, plus the production retainer. Below that, optimization load outruns the budget.

Patience for two weeks +

The first two weeks are for tracking and structure. Judging performance before that is judging noise.
